# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary users are potential new members who are evaluating gym options. They are looking for an aesthetic, modern environment that matches their fitness ambitions.

## Product Purpose
The website serves as a digital storefront to attract new members to Body Work Gym & Fitness in Cuautitlán Izcalli. Success is defined by effectively communicating the gym's value and converting visitors into members.

## Positioning
A high-performance and demanding environment ("Entrena Diferente"). It is positioned as a gym that demands as much from its members as they demand from themselves—no excuses, no limits.

## Operating Context
Visitors are evaluating the gym from personal devices (mobile or desktop) to decide where to invest their time and effort for fitness.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Built using Astro.
- The moving 3D dumbbell scene (Three.js) is a strict requirement and must be preserved at all costs.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Body Work Gym & Fitness
- Slogan: "Entrena Diferente"
- Tone: Bold, demanding, and modern.

## Product Principles
- **Aesthetic Attraction:** The design must be modern and visually striking to stand out to potential members.
- **Unapologetic Performance:** The interface should feel intense and focused, reflecting the demanding nature of the workouts.
- **Dynamic Presence:** Use motion and interactivity (like the 3D dumbbell) to make the experience feel active and alive.
